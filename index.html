Where the data is – and where it is missing

Each dot is a NASA test. The shaded band is the planned Moon-base atmosphere (≥ 30% O₂ at ≈ 70 kPa / 10.2 psi): the fewer dots inside it, the bigger the knowledge gap for lunar fire safety.
